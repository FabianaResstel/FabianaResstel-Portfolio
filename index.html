About

Building thoughtful, user-focused digital experiences with modern web technologies and AI-assisted development.

I’m a front-end developer with a professional background in food service and hospitality. Years of working in fast-paced environments taught me precision, adaptability, teamwork, and how to solve problems under pressure — skills I now bring to building thoughtful, user-focused web experiences.

My development toolkit includes HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Bootstrap, responsive design, APIs, Tailwind CSS, and modern AI-assisted development tools. I’m continuously expanding my skills through hands-on projects and real-world problem solving, with a focus on creating clean, responsive, and intuitive digital experiences.

One of my upcoming projects is The Line, a tool designed specifically for small restaurant business owners, head chefs, sous chefs, and kitchen managers. The idea comes directly from my experience in professional kitchens: identifying the tools, workflows, and information that can make a demanding kitchen environment more organized and efficient. Rather than building a project simply for a portfolio, I’m developing The Line around a real industry I understand, with the goal of turning that experience into useful technology. The project is currently in its early planning phase, with development beginning soon.

Bilingual in English and Portuguese, with dual U.S.–Brazilian citizenship, I bring a combination of technical skills, industry experience, and a practical, problem-solving mindset to every project I work on.
