La Paz Hostel

Designing a multilingual hostel experience around real user needs.

A responsive React website concept for a hostel in La Paz, Bolivia, designed to help travelers explore the property, rooms, activities, restaurants, and local information.

The project includes multilingual navigation, reusable components, React Router, room selection, check-in and check-out dates, and a simple booking experience. The interface combines practical information with a clean visual style inspired by the local environment.

Built with: React, JavaScript, Bootstrap, React Router, responsive design

The Line

Turning firsthand kitchen experience into a digital product idea.

The Line is an independent product project inspired by my experience working in professional kitchens. Instead of creating another portfolio project based only on a technical exercise, I wanted to explore a problem space I already understand.

The idea is an AI-assisted toolkit for restaurant owners, head chefs, sous chefs, and kitchen managers, bringing everyday tasks such as recipe scaling, food costing, prep planning, menu development, nutrition information, and recipe generation into one place.

The project is currently in the early development stage, with the focus on defining the product, its workflows, and the technology behind it.

Planned technology: React, TypeScript, APIs, AI-assisted development

About

Building thoughtful, user-focused digital experiences with modern web technologies and AI-assisted development.

I’m a front-end developer who brings a practical, adaptable mindset shaped by years of working in fast-paced professional environments. My experience taught me how to stay organized, pay attention to details, work well with others, and solve problems under pressure — skills I now apply to creating thoughtful, user-focused web experiences.

My development journey is hands-on and project-driven. I work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Bootstrap, Tailwind CSS, responsive design, APIs, and modern AI-assisted development tools. I enjoy turning ideas into clean, responsive, and intuitive digital experiences while continuously learning and improving along the way.

One of my upcoming projects is The Line, a tool designed for small restaurant business owners, head chefs, sous chefs, and kitchen managers. The idea comes directly from my experience in professional kitchens and from understanding the challenges of keeping a demanding kitchen organized and efficient. I want to build technology around a real industry I know, rather than creating a project simply for the sake of a portfolio.

Looking ahead, I’m also planning to combine web development with another passion: travel. I’m exploring opportunities to travel and experience different parts of the world while continuing to develop my skills and contribute through technology. As I travel, I plan to document the places I visit through YouTube videos and content on Instagram and TikTok, creating a personal project that brings together coding, travel, culture, and real-world experiences.

Bilingual in English and Portuguese, with Brazilian and American citizenship, I bring a combination of technical skills, hospitality experience, curiosity, and a practical problem-solving mindset to everything I build. I’m always open to new challenges, interesting ideas, and opportunities to create something useful.
